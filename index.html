Heat & Thermodynamics — Core Formulas

Key kinetic theory and thermodynamic equations demonstrated in this gas chamber (Inter Part-1, Ch. 6 — Heat and Thermodynamics):

1. Ideal Gas Equation of State PV = nRT = Nk_BT
2. Kinetic Pressure of an Ideal Gas P = ⅓ ρ ⟨v²⟩ = (N m ⟨v²⟩) / 3V
3. Translational Kinetic Energy & Temperature ⟨Eₖ⟩ = ½ m ⟨v²⟩ = ³⁄₂ k_BT
4. Root-Mean-Square (RMS) Molecular Speed v_rms = √(3k_BT / m) = √(3RT / M)
5. First Law of Thermodynamics Q = ΔU + W   (W = PΔV)
6. Isothermal vs. Adiabatic Curves PV = const  |  PVᵞ = const
Computational Engine: Spatial Hash Grid & Elastic Collisions ▼

Pairwise collision checks scale as O(N²). To maintain 60 FPS with hundreds of colliding molecules, the chamber is partitioned into a uniform Spatial Hash Grid (O(N) expected complexity) where each particle tests collisions only against its 3×3 neighborhood cells:

// Elastic Impulse Resolution Along Collision Normal n̂
j = −2 · ((v₂ − v₁) · n̂) / (1/m₁ + 1/m₂)
v₁′ = v₁ − (j / m₁) · n̂  |  v₂′ = v₂ + (j / m₂) · n̂
